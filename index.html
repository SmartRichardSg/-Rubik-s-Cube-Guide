🧩 Rubik's Cube Guide

Beginner method • Learn one step at a time

Your Progress

0 of 7 steps completed

1White Cross

Make a white cross on the white face. Also line up each side colour with its matching centre.

Goal

White cross + the four side colours correctly aligned.

2White Corners

Place each white corner into its correct position. Put the corner below its destination and repeat:

R' D' R D

Repeat the four-move sequence until the corner is correctly inserted.

3Middle Layer

Find a top-layer edge without yellow. Match its front colour with the centre.

Move edge to the right

U R U' R' U' F' U F

Move edge to the left

U' L' U L U F U' F'

4Yellow Cross

Hold the yellow face on top. Use this algorithm to progress from dot → L → line → cross:

F R U R' U' F'

You may need to repeat it. For an L shape, hold the L at the upper-left. For a line, hold it horizontally.

5Yellow Edges

Turn the top until at least one yellow edge matches its side centre. Hold the solved edge at the back and use:

R U R' U R U2 R'

Repeat as needed until all four yellow edges match their centres.

6Position Yellow Corners

Find a corner that is in the correct location, even if its yellow sticker is not facing up. Hold it at the front-right-top position.

U R U' L' U R' U' L

Repeat until all four yellow corners are in their correct locations.

7Finish the Yellow Face

Keep the cube in the same orientation. Put an unsolved yellow corner at the front-right-top.

R' D' R D

Repeat the sequence until that corner has yellow on top. Then turn only the top layer to bring the next unsolved corner into position. Do not rotate the whole cube.

Important: The cube may look scrambled during this step. Keep going and it will restore itself.

Move Notation

R — Right clockwise
R' — Right anti-clockwise
L — Left clockwise
L' — Left anti-clockwise
U — Up clockwise
U' — Up anti-clockwise
D — Down clockwise
D' — Down anti-clockwise
F — Front clockwise
F' — Front anti-clockwise
B — Back clockwise
B' — Back anti-clockwise
2 — Turn twice, e.g. U2

Clockwise means as you look directly at that face.
